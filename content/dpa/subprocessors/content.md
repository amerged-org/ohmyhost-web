# Provider register

Annex III to the ohmyho.st DPA. Version: November 3, 2026. The contracted entity and applicable service terms govern each engagement.

The selected capabilities determine which providers receive workload data. Provider data-centre, support and onward-processing arrangements are not established solely by selecting a region. Subprocessor changes follow the notice and objection procedure in the DPA.

Changes in this version (November 3, 2026): Orq.ai is added for AI requests routed through its service.

AI requests made through the gateway are routed through orq.ai to the provider serving the selected model. Our gateway does not persist prompt or response bodies. Orq and model providers may retain data under their applicable processing and retention terms. The AI jurisdiction setting does not make global edge execution, support or all other service processing EU-only.

Previous changes (October 30, 2026): Neon is removed from the register: the platform control databases for account, project, deployment and usage records moved to Cloudflare D1 (EU jurisdiction), and the Neon control databases and all retained pre-migration application databases were deleted. Contabo GmbH remains listed for active PGCF database infrastructure. New projects retain their US or EU database choice. Existing application databases migrated with their owner’s authorization use US infrastructure; retained R2 objects remain at their recorded locations. Cloudflare stores database backups and SQL export archives in R2. Earlier project SES, Route 53 and AWS export resources remain retired; Resend can use its own AWS infrastructure.

## Account, business and customer-selected services

WorkOS supports ohmyho.st account identity and authorisation. Stripe handles enabled payments, subscriptions, invoices and tax calculation. GitHub supplies authorised source repositories. Mintlify delivers public documentation, search and the documentation assistant, including visitor requests and submitted search/assistant text; it does not receive application databases or runtime secrets as part of documentation hosting. They process relevant account/business data in the roles set out in their applicable terms and our Privacy notice; they are not automatically recipients of every customer's application database or files. [WorkOS DPA](https://workos.com/legal/data-processing-addendum) · [Stripe DPA](https://stripe.com/legal/dpa) · [Mintlify privacy](https://www.mintlify.com/legal/privacy).

A customer's own auth provider, coding agent, automation service or external integration is selected and contracted by that customer unless expressly included in an ohmyho.st order.
