By [Sebastian Mertens](https://www.linkedin.com/in/auto-mate/) · November 4, 2026

ohmyhost is now available as a plugiin in ChatGPT. You can build a web app right in the chat, preview it, and publish the tested version. Describe your project, connect your ohmyhost account, and see  what your agent comes up with.

## From Chat to Your Own App

A small tool, a dashboard , or a website: Just tell ChatGPT what you need. The plugin can save your app’s files and their versions to ohmyhost. You can continuue working in the chat later, test changes, or restore a previous version. If your project is already on GitHub, you can also use that repository.

To give it a try , all you need is a secific task:

```text
Build a small web app to manage my reading list. Show me a preview first. Publish it only after I have checked the preview and asked you to publish.
```

Open the preview and check whether you can add or remove  books. Then request the next revisin or publication.

The  plugin supprts Vite, Next.js, and TanStack Start. You'll need an ohmyhost account and available hosting credits. You decide which changes and releases your agent is allowed to make.
