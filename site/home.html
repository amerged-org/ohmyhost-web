Why this exists.

Short version: I got tired of paying five bills.

SaaS got expensive, and every month you pay a surcharge for — what, exactly?

Nobody was doing the obvious thing: buy the good services in bulk, bundle them, sell them at Aldi prices. So I did that. We add a small margin on what the services underneath cost us. That's the whole business model.

No lock-in, because I care whether this is actually good. Unhappy? Take your database and go. Happy? Stay and pay for what you use. Your credits work on every project you have, not one.

And you don't get a dashboard. Ask Codex, Claude, whatever you run — our MCP hands back your usage and your data. No UI. This was built for agents.
