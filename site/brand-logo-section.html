03 — logo

The Ω mark and ohmyho.st wordmark are one identity. The wordmark uses JetBrains Mono at weight 400; the mark works on its own at small sizes. Always spell the brand ohmyho.st. The short forms are omh and omh.st.

ohmyho.st

Construction, space and size

  • The mark uses a 100 × 100 grid, a 12.5-unit stroke, rounded caps and joins.
  • Leave at least one stroke width clear on every side.
  • At 20 pixels or smaller, use the thicker 15-unit favicon variant.
  • Keep the original proportions; use the light mark on dark surfaces and the dark mark on light surfaces.
  • Do not rotate, distort, outline, add gradients or combine the mark with a cube.
